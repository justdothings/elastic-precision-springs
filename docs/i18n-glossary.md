## Translation Notes

- Bulgarian is the primary content language and should be edited first.
- German terminology varies by region and exact product: “Schenkelfeder” is common for leg torsion springs, while “Torsionsfeder” is broader.
- Italian “nastro trasportatore a rete metallica” is clearer for buyer search intent than a literal spring-belt translation.
- Use decimal comma in German/Italian/Bulgarian body copy where natural: 4,5 mm. URLs keep ASCII slugs.
